A VERY SHORT INTRODUCTION ↓

Mirna thought she'd caught her big break when she entered the world of New York City fine dining. And then she totally beefed it.

Left with no other choice, she crawls back home, ready to resign herself to the small life she's always dreaded. But before she can give up entirely, Henry Rockmore comes storming into town. The white celebrity chef is famous for "elevating" Mexican cuisine (and he's been Mirna's idol since childhood).

Mirna claws her way into Rockmore's orbit, convinced that under his wing, things will finally work out the way they're supposed to. But it isn't long before she discovers that Rockmore has manipulated her to burnish his progressive credentials. Worse, she's just the latest in a long line of people he's used and discarded on his way to the top.

Now Mirna faces a choice: pay an unbearable price to stay close to power or let go of everything she thought she wanted. She's spent her life trying to win at a game designed to erase her. Maybe it's time for her to stop playing.
