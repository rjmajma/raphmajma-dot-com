A VERY SHORT INTRODUCTION ↓

Mirna wants to make it in New York fine dining. She thinks working for a celebrity chef is her big break.

Unfortunately, being close to the spotlight isn't the same thing as being in it. One Star is a novel about ambition, family, and what happens when someone else decides whose story gets told.
